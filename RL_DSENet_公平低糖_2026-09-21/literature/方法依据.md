# 文献与本次适配

1. Patek et al., Modular Closed-Loop Control of Diabetes, IEEE TBME 2012. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4607512/ ; DOI 10.1109/TBME.2012.2192930. III-A/B：10min CGM均值 +17×30min最小二乘斜率；风险rho，phi=1/(1+rho)，下降时衰减，非下降时解除。完整原方法含依赖临床CF的IOB修正与限额；本轮CGM-only版本不使用隐藏CF，不称完整SSM复现。17min是组织扩散滞后补偿，不应误称已验证的未来17min预测。
2. Predictive Low-Glucose Insulin Suspension Reduces Duration of Nocturnal Hypoglycemia in Children Without Increasing Ketosis, Diabetes Care 2015. https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/38/7/1197/30982/Predictive-Low-Glucose-Insulin-Suspension-Reduces 。Kalman 30min预测<80、当前<230暂停，当前≤70暂停，首次CGM上升恢复；与旧等80/90恢复规则不同。未在当前成人全天任务直接复刻夜间暂停时限。
3. Hughes et al., Hypoglycemia Prevention via Pump Attenuation and Red-Yellow-Green “Traffic” Lights Using Continuous Glucose Monitoring and Insulin Pump Data, JDST 2010. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2956822/ 。群体模型observer为备选，附录矩阵完整但Kalman符号/初始化不充分，未作为无适配精确复现。
4. OpenAPS reference design https://openaps.org/reference-design/ 与 https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openaps/oref0/master/lib/determine-basal/determine-basal.js 。活性胰岛素必须由实际交付历史计算，correction与meal insulin需区分；本任务没有可读取的临床CF，不能拿sim隐藏参数代替。
5. TSODE ISBI2026，https://doi.org/10.1109/ISBI61048.2026.11515483 与 https://arxiv.org/html/2511.12417v1 。动作条件预测与风险校准；bolus/meal条件不同，不直接混入basal-only表。
6. Kwon et al. 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21094v1 ，代码 https://github.com/safe-autonomy-lab/GlucoAlg 。统一有/无shield比较可借鉴；含rescue carbs等不同动作，未冒充本次同setting重现基线。

不把文献报告的临床收益移植为本项目结果。所有新效果来自独立sim闭环。
